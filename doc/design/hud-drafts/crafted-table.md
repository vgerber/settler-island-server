# Crafted Table (direction B)

Every control is a physical table object. The table grows wooden and clay fixtures around the island and almost no 2D
remains. Mockups: `crafted-table-build.png` (main turn, build mode) and `crafted-table-trade.png` (trade offer), both
1600x900 over the real board screenshots; sources `crafted-table-*.svg`, generated by `tools-ct/gen.py`.
(The mockups use Lato as a stand-in for Atkinson Hyperlegible and Bitter, which are not installed for inkscape.)

## 1. Mood

A game night at a wooden table, lit by one warm lamp. The island stays the hero; around it sit a walnut hand tray, a
clay build slab, a brass bell, a bank rack with five card piles and a dev deck, and a seat plate at each opponent's
tray. Nothing is a window: no panel, no cream paper, no grey button. State is carried by height, light and tokens:
the acting seat is raised and lit, seats that owe an answer carry a token, a sign at the table's far corner says what
to do. Two materials only, walnut (fixtures) and clay (the build slab), plus brass for the bell and name labels.
Warmth stays; the uncanny parts (two worlds, stickers, grey fade) go.

Why it works, and why it is ours: a physical control tells its use by its shape (a bell ends a turn, a pile of cards
is a hand), a cost is shown as the cards that pay it, and a missing resource is shown where it is missing. Nothing
here is taken from an existing UI; the vocabulary is generic tabletop furniture.

### Adapting to 1280x720
- Same anchors in a proportional 16:9 layout (scale 0.8), with three exceptions that keep sizes above the floor:
  hand cards stay at 66 x 90 (scale 0.92, not 0.8) and the tray grows by 12 px; cost chips stay 13 x 18 (tooltip and
  reading spot show them large); all 2D text stays at least 13 px (sign 26, plates 16, ticker 13).
- Default camera pulls the island up by about 28 px at 720p (it has spare sea at the bottom) so the hand never
  covers a hex.
- Awards board and bank rack shrink to 0.85; the ticker drops to two lines.
- Other aspect ratios: the fixtures anchor to the table edges (letterbox with table texture), not to the window.
- Web: every hover read has a tap or long-press equivalent; the reading spot opens on tap.

## 3. Every element

**Own hand.** Five fixed slots as stacks (clay, wood, sheep, wheat, ore), 72 x 96 cards, up to 6 layers (2.5 px right,
5 px up per layer), count chip at the top-right corner of the top card (tile-chip style, brown rim). A slot with 0
cards is a dashed hollow in its colour, never `0`. Hover lifts the stack 16 px with a 3 px lantern rim (about 150 ms);
click selects (lift 24 px). Picking: one ray against the resting pose (card plus 6 px margin), topmost wins, 80 ms
hysteresis; a camera drag never plays a card. The tray lives in a camera-fixed rig (second `SubViewport` camera
sharing the world, or a `Node3D` locked to the camera) so orbiting never moves it. Card face: terrain colour from
`Palette.RESOURCE_COLORS`, rim from `RESOURCE_PATTERN_COLORS`, one embossed glyph (bricks, log end, fleece, ear,
peak), a lighter top-left facet and a darker bottom-right facet, 6 rounded corners; faces carry no words. Back: walnut
with a brass diamond.

**Reading spot.** After 0.25 s of hover (or on tap) an enlarged copy (140 x 190) with one line of text appears at a
fixed spot above Lin's plate (x 30-190, y 640-800, sea only, never over the board). It shows name, count and what it
pays for (`Clay: road, settlement`).

**Dev cards.** A fan at the tray's right end (x 650-830): stacked identical cards (Knight x2 with a count chip), the
card bought this turn sits 14 px lower with a `NEW` tag, and cannot be played until next turn. Face: parchment with a
plum border, one cut corner (dev cards are told from resources by shape and border, not colour alone), one pictogram
(shield, coins, star). Playable cards lift 6 px on their own; unplayable stay down, not faded. Playing one flips it
face up at the table centre for 1.5 s, then it drops onto a played pile at the seat.

**Own status.** The plate at the tray's left end: seat tab, diamond, name, `7` in a gold-rim chip, `of 10 points`.
Pieces left appear only on the build slab: a `n left` tag on a piece when 2 or fewer remain (city), full numbers in the
reading spot on hover. `pieces 15/5/4` is gone.

**Opponents (seat stations).** Name plate with seat-colour stripe, solid seat shape (circle, square, triangle,
diamond), name, robot glyph instead of `(bot)`, points as the one big gold-rim numeral. On the tray: one card back per
card up to 8, then a `+N` chip; the count chip always shows the number. Dev cards: plum backs beside the tray with a
chip. Knights: small shield tokens in a row (none = zero). Longest road and Largest army: plaques that slide from the
award board to the holder's plate with a 0.4 s camera nudge. Active seat: plate and tray raised 6 px with a lantern
glow and a slow 1.4 s pulse of the emblem; other seats sit low and 20% dim. Waiting and answering: a round token drops
onto the plate (three dots = waiting, X = declined, tick = accepted, scale = offering) and lifts when answered.

**Bank.** The rack above, 6 piles with count chips (chips shown always; they are 25 px and cheap). When a count is 3
or fewer the chip turns amber and the pile shrinks accordingly. Bought and paid cards fly between bank and hand in
0.4 s.

**Prompt.** The sign. Verb first, 5 words at most: `Roll the dice`, `Place a road`, `Discard 4`, `Answer Bot 2's trade`,
`Waiting for Bot 2`. Tab in the acting seat's colour and shape (ivory tab for charcoal gets an ivory rim). Phase names
(`Main`) never appear. One state field drives sign, seat light, tokens and bell.

**Actions with costs and disabled states.** The build slab: road, settlement, city, card deck, trade token. Under each
piece its cost as mini cards (14 x 19, colour plus glyph, repeated for 2 or 3). States:
- affordable: full colour; hover lifts the piece 9 px and lifts the paying cards in the hand 16 px with `-1` tags
  (build mock: road lifts clay and wood);
- cannot afford: the piece sits 5 px lower, still full colour, the missing mini card is a red dashed outline and a red
  tag says `need 1 Ore` (build mock: city);
- not now (other seat's turn, trade open): the slab is covered by a wooden lid with one line, `Answer the trade
  first` (trade mock); the bell is tucked under a cover with `Bot 2's turn`;
- never a grey wash.
Click enters the mode (sign changes, targets appear). End turn is the bell: always visible on your turn, a 6 px
brass glow only when nothing else useful remains, one click, no confirm.

**Trade offer and answers.** A mat slides 0.35 s out of the offerer's side (here from Bot 2) onto the lower-left sea:
left half `Bot 2 gives` with real 3D cards (2 Wood) and a count chip, an inlaid brass arrow, right half `You give`
(1 Wheat). Accept (teal, 148 x 52) and Decline (ember, 130 x 52) are full-colour wood buttons 24 px apart, both at
the mat's near edge; the line under them says who is still deciding. Tokens on the seats show the answers (Bot 1
waiting, Bot 3 declined). Our own hand shows the effect: the wheat stack lifted, the empty wood slot with two ghost
cards `+2`. Building an offer uses the same mat: click or drag cards from the tray to the mat, pick wants from the
bank rack; bank and port trades use the mat with the rate as a number on the bank edge.

**Discard.** No dialog. The tray cards lift on hover; the sign says `Discard 4 (2 of 4)`; picked cards rise 24 px with
a red `-` tag; `Confirm` appears as a wood button on the tray's rim when the count matches. The table dims 30% except
the robber's tile and the tray, so the cause of the 7 stays visible. Seats that owe a discard carry a `discard n`
token.

**Dice result.** The 3D dice show the roll; the gold-rim total chip floats above the tray (`8`). Production: chips of
matching tiles pulse once, one card per resource arcs from each producing building to its seat (`+1`), 120 ms apart,
at most 1.2 s per roll. Blocked tiles pulse dark.

**Log.** Ticker plus drawer. The ticker shows the last 2-3 events (seat shape, verb, objects), fading after about 6 s.
The history drawer (peg or key L) lists one row per player turn (`Lin: rolled 8, got 1 ore, built a road`); rows click
to replay their marker. Errors are not events: the refused control shakes and a one-line `#F08A78` message shows
for 2 s next to it.

**Settings.** The gear peg opens a small walnut panel in the table's corner (same plates, flat controls in cream on
walnut), with volume, quality (Compatibility or High), animation speed (normal, fast, instant), colour-blind aid
(adds a hatch to seat colours), clean view. It is the one 2D panel that stays.

**Game over.** Title sign (`Bot 2 wins`, 64 px display face) drops in at the top centre over a 40% dimmed board; the
seat stations rise and rank themselves: winner's plate lit and lifted, points numerals large, others at their order.
`Play again` and `Leave` are two wood buttons under the sign. No stats list.

**Placement targets.** Ghost pieces of the real model in the acting seat's colour at 80% opacity with a 2.8 px lantern
outline, breathing over 1.4 s; roads as strips on the edge. A 10-15% scrim dims the rest. Hover turns the ghost solid
and lifts it, pulses the neighbouring number chips and floats tiny resource icons above the spot; port spots show
`2:1 wood`. Robber mode raises legal tiles 4 px and dims the rest; the robber carries a lantern spotlight ring. Own
pieces get a thin seat-colour base ring (an ivory rim for the charcoal seat). The thick gold rings are deleted.

Fonts: two faces with fixed roles. Display (`Bitter-Bold`): sign 30 px, points numerals, bell caption, game-over title.
Sans (`AtkinsonHyperlegible-Bold` / Regular): plates 20 px, ticker 15, captions 13, buttons 22. Five sizes: 64 (title),
30 (sign), 22 (numbers, buttons), 16-20 (labels), 13 (caption minimum). Numbers: the `zero` OpenType feature off
(plain zero, no slash) and `tnum` on via `FontVariation`; check 0, 6, 8 at 14 px. One chip language for every number
(tile-chip hexagon: off-white, dark digit, 2.5 px rim and a contact shadow); gold rim for points, brown for counts,
plum for dev. 2D text over sea or hexes sits on a dark pill or has a 3 px ink outline; a headless check fails any
label under 13 px or under 4.5:1.

## 5. Motion

One grammar for events: anticipation 150 ms (source lifts), travel 400-600 ms ease-out, settle 150 ms with a small
squash, count-up; at most 1.2 s per event, click skips, the speed setting (normal, fast, instant) scales all of it.
Persistent state is static; only events and the one expected action animate. Specific cues:
- turn start: acting seat rises 6 px and its lantern glow fades in over 0.3 s; the sign text swaps with a 120 ms slide;
- card flights between bank, hand and seats; steal: a back lifts from the victim's stack and flies to the thief;
- build: piece drops with a dust puff and a seat-colour ring fading over 0.6 s;
- awards: plaque slides to the holder, the camera nudges 2 degrees toward the seat;
- tokens drop on arrival and lift on answer;
- the ghost breath (1.4 s) is the only looping animation on the board besides the active-seat pulse.

## 6. How it builds in Godot

3D (new):
- `scripts/table/` fixtures as `Node3D` scenes built in code like the existing board props: `HandTray`, `CardStack`
  (shared `ArrayMesh` card with vertex colours, glyph in a 256 px atlas, blob shadow quad alpha 0.35 that grows with
  lift, 6-12 triangles per card, `MultiMesh` for stacks and backs), `BuildSlab`, `Bell`, `BankRack`, `SeatStation`,
  `TradeMat`, `StandingSign`, `AwardBoard`.
- The hand rig: camera-fixed `Node3D` under the camera so orbiting never moves it; picking by a ray against resting
  colliders (`Area3D`, `BoxShape3D`, resting pose only).
- Ghost targets reuse the real piece meshes with an unshaded alpha material and a breathing `Tween`; scrim is one
  full-table dark quad.
- Count chips and sign text: `Label3D` with `billboard` and a pill mesh, or a small `SubViewport` per fixture drawn
  once; Compatibility safe (no shadow maps per card, shared materials, few lights).
- Seat shapes appear on piece caps, plates, sign tabs, ticker and tokens from one `SeatStyle` helper.

2D (kept, small): `Control` with `StyleBoxFlat` (walnut and brass, 6-8 px radius, 2 px lighter top border) for the
settings panel, history drawer, game-over buttons and the clean-view/key hints; `NinePatchRect` is not needed.
Shaders: one tiny `canvas_item` shader for the lantern glow; one `spatial` unshaded alpha shader for ghosts; no
post-processing.

Files in `client/godot/scripts/hud/`:
- delete: `hud_top_bar`, `hud_action_bar`, `hud_action_list`, `hud_dice`, `hud_hourglass`, `hud_glow`, `hud_resource_card`,
  `hud_resource_icon`, `hud_resource_picker`, `hud_dev_row`, `hud_hand`, `hud_player_panel`, `hud_players`, `hud_seat_chip`,
  `hud_bank_pane`, `hud_own_summary`, `hud_waiting`, `hud_turn_banner`, `hud_trade_panel`, `hud_trade_builder`,
  `hud_trade_dialog`, `hud_discard_dialog` (replaced by 3D objects fed from the same state);
- rewrite: `hud.gd` (state fan-out to fixtures), `hud_toasts` into the ticker, `hud_event_log` into the history drawer,
  `hud_legal` into the ghost-target controller, `hud_game_over`, `hud_settings_popup`, `hud_gear_button` (peg),
  `hud_dialog`, `hud_text`, `hud_ui`, `seat_names`;
- theme: `build_theme.gd` shrinks to the walnut panel, brass button and chip styles; `palette.gd` gains `LANTERN`, `DEV`,
  `POINTS_GOLD`; `Palette.TARGET` is retired.

Also changes the board scripts: the seat trays get seat colours and a station anchor; the camera gets the default
frame, a Home key reset and the compass icon shown only after a camera move.

## 7. Effort

Large. About 6 agent tasks of about 30 minutes each, in sequence for shared files: (1) palette, fonts, chip style and
camera rig, (2) card meshes, stack, hand tray with picking and reading spot, (3) build slab, bell, prompt sign and
ticker, (4) seat stations with tokens, awards and bank rack, (5) trade mat with discard on the tray, (6) ghost targets,
settings, game over, headless label checks and the 1280x720 pass. Plus a verifier playtest and screenshot review per
wave. The most modelling work of the four directions.

## 8. Risks

- **Busy table.** Eight fixtures around the island. Mitigation: they sit on sea, tokens appear only with events, the
  ticker is two lines, key H hides all 2D labels, and the scene must pass a squint test (turn, waiting and targets
  readable at 50% blur).
- **Card size at 1280x720.** Cards at 66 x 90 need the camera nudge; if the island cannot move, the tray must overlap
  the bottom of the table by about 10 px.
- **Text on tilted faces.** Faces carry colour and one glyph only; every number is a screen-facing chip.
- **Hover hitbox mismatch** if colliders follow the lifted pose; they must not.
- **Diegetic overreach.** The sign is a fallback if a cue is missed; the active seat, the tokens and the sign always
  agree because one state field drives them.
- **Compatibility and web budget.** `MultiMesh`, shared materials, no per-card shadows; measure on the web export.
- **Charcoal seat on dark wood.** Needs the ivory rim everywhere (emblem, tab, ghost outline); checked in the mockups.
- **Mockup caveats.** Target positions are illustrative, the right tray is recoloured over the screenshot, and the
  baked-in Reset view button is covered by the sign.

## 9. What is dropped

Top bar, right column and bottom bar; the log panel; beige and cream paper panels and grey buttons; 2D dice and `= 8`;
the `pieces`, `knights`, `Dev` strings; slashed zeros; faded grey disabled states; the gold target rings; the floating
Reset view button (now a key and a conditional compass); the `(bot)` suffix; the discard, trade and steal dialogs over
the board; phase names. Kept 2D: the settings panel, the history drawer and the game-over buttons.
